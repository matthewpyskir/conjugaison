# Conjugaison

French conjugation flashcards: the 500 most common verbs plus 32 reflexive verbs, every tense, and past participles.

Open the site in Safari on iPhone, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. It works offline after the first visit.

Conjugations come from [Lefff](https://www.labri.fr/perso/clement/lefff/) (Inria, LGPL-LR) via the `french-verbs-lefff` package. Verb frequency is based on the OpenSubtitles French word list ([hermitdave/FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords)). Fonts: Cormorant Garamond and Jost (SIL Open Font License).
